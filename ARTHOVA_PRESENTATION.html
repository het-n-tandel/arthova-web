QVM Multi-Factor Quantitative Model
Formulaic equity scoring: Quality (40%), Valuation (30%), Momentum (30%)
Composite Formula
S_QVM = 0.40 * S_Quality + 0.30 * S_Valuation + 0.30 * S_Momentum
Evaluates equities on an objective 0 to 100 point scale.
Quality Sub-Factor (40%)
Blends Return on Capital Employed (ROCE), Return on Equity (ROE), Debt-to-Equity ratio, and the Stanford Piotroski F-Score.
Valuation Sub-Factor (30%)
Calculates P/E discount relative to sector median, Price-to-Book (P/B), and trailing Dividend Yield.
Momentum Sub-Factor (30%)
Evaluates 3M, 6M, and 12M relative price strength normalized against the benchmark Nifty 50 index.
💡 Classification: 80-100: Strong Buy | 65-79: Buy/Accumulate | 50-64: Hold | <50: Underperform/Avoid 7 / 18

Stanford Piotroski 9-Point F-Score Engine
Algorithmic balance sheet health audit across 9 fundamental signals
Profitability Signals (4 Pts)
1. Positive ROA
2. Positive Operating Cash Flow (CFO)
3. Increase in ROA year-over-year
4. Accruals check: CFO > Net Income
Leverage & Liquidity (3 Pts)
5. Lower Long-Term Debt ratio
6. Improvement in Current Ratio
7. Zero equity dilution (no new shares issued)
Operating Efficiency (2 Pts)
8. Expansion in Gross Profit Margin
9. Higher Asset Turnover Ratio year-over-year
💡 Scoring Threshold: 8-9 points = Stellar Fundamentals | 5-7 points = Average | 0-4 points = High Risk 8 / 18

Altman Z-Score Bankruptcy Distress Classifier
Protecting investor capital from corporate insolvency and default
Multi-Ratio Discriminant
Z = 1.2 * X1 + 1.4 * X2 + 3.3 * X3 + 0.6 * X4 + 0.999 * X5
Synthesizes liquidity, cumulative profitability, productivity, leverage, and sales.
Safe Zone (Z > 2.99)
Minimal financial distress; strong solvency buffers; low probability of default within 24 months.
Grey Zone (1.81 <= Z <= 2.99)
Moderate financial stress; warrants close fundamental monitoring and strict position sizing.
Distress Zone (Z < 1.81)
Severe bankruptcy risk; automatic warning badge generated to prompt position reduction.
💡 Risk Safety: Integrated with the stock deep-dive page to immediately flag distressed balance sheets. 9 / 18
